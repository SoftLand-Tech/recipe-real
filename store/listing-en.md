## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```text
Recipes say "25 minutes". Dinner says otherwise.

RecipeReal is a recipe app built around one idea: honest cook times.
In guided Cook Mode, a stopwatch runs step by step while you cook, so the app
measures what a recipe really takes — then shows the claimed time next to the
measured reality and calibrates every estimate to your pace.

WHAT YOU GET

• Honest cook times — every recipe shows the time it claims next to what it
  really takes, measured by real cooks in Cook Mode (AI estimates are shown
  until cooks have measured a recipe).
• Guided Cook Mode — a full-screen, step-by-step stopwatch with step alarms,
  pause/resume, and per-step timing. Alarms and timing keep working with the
  screen locked.
• Calibrated to you — the more you cook, the closer every estimate gets to your
  kitchen, not an average one.
• A library of 20,000+ recipes across categories for weeknight dinners,
  gatherings, and everything between.
• Works offline — the library downloads to your phone, so browsing and cooking
  work with no signal.
• English and Arabic — the full app, with complete right-to-left support.
• A community of cooks — share what you tried, like and comment, and follow
  kitchens you trust. Every cook you log sharpens the estimates for everyone.

FREE TO USE

RecipeReal is free to download and use. An optional Premium tier is planned;
checkout is not live in this release.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Delete your account at any time from Settings in the app (Danger zone), or on
the web: https://recipe-real.softland.tech/account/delete

Questions, feedback, or a correction about a recipe? Write to us:
https://recipe-real.softland.tech/contact
```
