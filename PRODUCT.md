# RecipeReal — Product Truth

## What it is
RecipeReal is a halal-only, bilingual (EN/AR, RTL) Android recipe app whose core
mechanism is **honest cook times**: recipes habitually claim short times; RecipeReal
measures what real cooks actually spend with a stopwatch in guided Cook Mode, then
shows the claim next to the measured reality ("Really takes") and calibrates every
estimate to the individual user's pace.

Assumptions (no interview available; inferred from the build session record):
- The owner is the sole operator; no marketing team, no external copywriting.
- Primary audience: Muslim home cooks (explicitly halal-only product), Egypt/MENA
  first, cooking for family on weeknights; Arabic-first households are common.
- Distribution is DIRECT (no Google Play): APK via recipe-real.softland.tech/download,
  APKPure possible. No store listing copy exists.

## Facts that must stay true on any surface
- Library: ~20,089 recipes, 137,644 steps, 26,771 steps carry a parsed time.
- Halal screening: explicit pork & alcohol rules applied to community content
  (posts, comments, notes, user recipes) and AI-published imports; the base
  imported library is not individually screened.
- Offline: the library downloads to the phone; cooking works with no signal.
- Cook Mode: full-screen step-by-step stopwatch, step alarms, pause/resume,
  per-step measurement; feeds the Reality calibration.
- Community: posts ("tries"), likes, comments, follows, public share pages.
- Free tier + paused Premium ($5.99/mo, $49.99/yr) — checkout not live yet.

- Owner's explicit landing brief: mind-blowing/overwhelming, no AI slop, no emojis
  (clean drawn icons), SEO + AEO heavy. Type energy: bold, confident; conflicting
  "BIG/no BIG texts" instruction — resolved as large-but-controlled display scale.
- One public user exists (the owner); do not invent testimonials, user counts, or

## Who the landing must convince
A Muslim home cook (phone, evening, hungry family) who has been burned by recipes
that say "25 minutes" and take an hour. They must understand the claim-vs-reality
mechanism in one viewport, trust it is built for them (halal-first), and install
the APK.
